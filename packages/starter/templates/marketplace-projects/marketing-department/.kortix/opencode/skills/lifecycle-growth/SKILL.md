# Lifecycle Growth

Use this skill for email, nurture, onboarding, activation, reactivation,
expansion, lead follow-up, customer education, and lifecycle experiments.

## Journey Map

Define:

- audience and eligibility,
- trigger,
- desired behavior,
- user promise,
- sequence or touchpoints,
- suppression rules,
- exit criteria,
- handoff owner,
- metric and guardrail.

## Experiment Brief

For each experiment:

- hypothesis,
- audience,
- variant,
- success metric,
- guardrail metric,
- sample or exposure caveat,
- implementation owner,
- rollback condition,
- approval needed.

## QA Checklist

Before any send or automation change:

- audience and suppressions reviewed,
- personalization tokens safe,
- links and UTMs checked,
- unsubscribe/compliance present,
- cadence and timezone reasonable,
- no unsupported claims,
- approval recorded.

## Safety

Never send, schedule, import contacts, change audiences, or alter production
automation without explicit approval.

## Output

End with a journey map, sequence draft, experiment brief, QA checklist, or
performance readout.
