# Campaign Strategy

Use this skill for integrated campaigns, launches, promotions, webinars, reports,
events, announcements, partner campaigns, and paid/owned distribution plans.

## Campaign Brief

Create campaign briefs with:

- objective and business metric,
- target audience and segment,
- insight/problem,
- offer or core message,
- channel plan,
- asset list,
- landing page or repo needs,
- lifecycle touches,
- distribution plan,
- timeline and owner map,
- measurement plan,
- risks and approval gates.

## Channel Planning

For each channel, state:

- audience fit,
- message angle,
- asset needed,
- tracking/UTM requirement,
- owner,
- launch dependency,
- approval needed.

## Launch Readiness

Before saying a campaign is ready, check:

- landing page exists and matches the promise,
- forms/conversion path work,
- analytics events and UTMs are defined,
- lifecycle follow-up is ready,
- sales/support enablement is ready if relevant,
- brand/legal/product claims are approved,
- budget/spend policy is explicit.

## Safety

Do not launch, schedule, send, publish, change budgets, or contact anyone
without approval. Draft and plan freely.

## Output

End with a launch checklist and the smallest next action.
