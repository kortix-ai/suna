# Content Engine

Use this skill for editorial calendar, blog, docs, landing pages, newsletters,
social copy, case studies, ebooks, webinars, scripts, and content refreshes.

## Brief Standard

Every brief should include:

- target audience and funnel stage,
- search/category/social/customer insight,
- job-to-be-done,
- angle and promise,
- outline,
- required proof points,
- internal links or supporting assets,
- distribution plan,
- measurement goal,
- approval owner.

## Refresh Workflow

For existing content:

1. Identify the asset, owner, intent, and current role in the funnel.
2. Review performance if connected; otherwise inspect public quality, freshness,
   positioning, internal links, and competitor alternatives.
3. Recommend keep/update/consolidate/retire.
4. Draft changes only when evidence is sufficient.
5. Do not publish without approval.

## Repurposing

Turn high-signal assets into:

- newsletter sections,
- social posts,
- sales snippets,
- landing page proof sections,
- nurture emails,
- webinar or event talking points.

## Brand And Claims

Load `brand-positioning` for voice, claims, and competitor comparisons. Mark any
unsupported claim as needing evidence.

## Output

End with a calendar item, brief, draft, refresh plan, or distribution pack.
