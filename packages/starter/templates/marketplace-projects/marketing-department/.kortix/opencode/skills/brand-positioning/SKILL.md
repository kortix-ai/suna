# Brand Positioning

Use this skill for positioning, messaging, brand voice, competitor narrative,
launch messaging, page copy review, and response drafts.

## Inputs

Collect or infer:

- ICP and buyer/persona,
- category and alternative solutions,
- core promise,
- proof points and evidence,
- competitor set,
- tone/voice constraints,
- claims that require legal/product approval,
- channel and asset type.

## Positioning Frame

Produce or maintain:

- audience,
- problem,
- old way/new way,
- differentiated promise,
- reasons to believe,
- proof points,
- objections,
- words to use and words to avoid.

## Claims Rules

Do not invent customer quotes, logos, benchmarks, rankings, revenue impact,
security/compliance claims, or competitor comparisons. If a claim is not backed
by a source, mark it as a draft claim that needs evidence.

## Competitor Watch

When reviewing competitors, capture:

- positioning and category language,
- offer and pricing changes,
- launches and feature claims,
- proof points,
- channels and content themes,
- likely implication for {{projectName}},
- recommended response.

## Output

End with one of:

- positioning brief,
- messaging map,
- copy critique,
- launch narrative,
- competitor response memo,
- claims/evidence checklist.
