# Marketing Analytics

Use this skill for performance reporting, KPI definitions, funnel analysis,
campaign readouts, anomaly detection, attribution caveats, and executive
summaries.

## Metrics Map

Prefer company-defined metrics from memory. If missing, propose definitions for:

- traffic by channel,
- landing page conversion,
- lead/demo/signup conversion,
- qualified pipeline or opportunity creation,
- email engagement and conversion,
- paid spend, CAC, CPL, ROAS where connected,
- content assisted conversion,
- retention/activation lifecycle metrics where connected.

## Readout Standard

Every readout should include:

- date range,
- source system,
- metric definition,
- baseline/comparison,
- observed movement,
- likely drivers,
- confidence,
- recommended action,
- data gaps.

## Anomaly Workflow

For drops/spikes:

1. Confirm source, date range, and tracking health.
2. Check recent repo/site changes, campaigns, spend changes, email sends, and
   external events.
3. Separate measurement breakage from real performance movement.
4. Escalate P0/P1 items with owner-ready next steps.

## Experiment Readout

Never overclaim. Include sample caveats, time window, segment effects, and
whether the experiment should ship, iterate, or stop.

## Output

End with a KPI table, anomaly note, experiment readout, or executive summary.
