# Rubric — research dossier (`02-research/`)

| criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `coverage` | Key questions unanswered | All key questions answered | Answers plus the surprising details that make great beats |
| `sourcing` | Blogs / content farms | Reputable secondary sources | Most non-trivial claims backed by primary sources (papers, agencies, datasets, textbooks) |
| `quotes` | Paraphrases | Quotes for most claims | Verbatim, locatable quotes for every claim (page/section/figure) |
| `nuance` | Oversimplified | Caveats noted | Uncertainty, competing views and common errors mapped explicitly |
| `numbers` | Unitless or unsourced | Sourced | Sourced, unit-checked, with tangible comparisons pre-computed and shown |
| `visual_leads` | None | Some | Dossier notes diagrams, data and analogies the art director can use |

Auto-fail: any claim without a source; a quote that isn't verbatim; Wikipedia as the only support for
a non-trivial claim; any source the researcher could not actually open.
