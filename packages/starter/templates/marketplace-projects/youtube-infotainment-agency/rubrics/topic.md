# Rubric — topic & editorial framing (`01-brief.json`)

| criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `question` | Vague subject ("black holes") | A clear question | A question the audience didn't know they had, that the video can answer completely |
| `misconception` | None identified | A plausible misconception | A widespread, specific wrong model the video will visibly replace |
| `originality` | Rehash of top results | Different emphasis | An angle, payoff or visual idea absent from the surveyed coverage |
| `payoff` | Answer is a definition | Answer is satisfying | Answer reframes something the viewer sees every day; has a memorable "number" |
| `feasibility` | Needs footage/people we can't make | Possible with effort | Plays to code-rendered animation; sources clearly available |
| `fit` | Off-niche | On-niche | Builds on what memory/ says our audience responds to |

Auto-fail: fewer than 3 existing videos surveyed; topic requires depicting real people or events
photorealistically; medical/legal/financial advice framing; the answer is contested and the brief
presents it as settled.
