# Rubric — storyboard & style frames (`04-visual/`)

| criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `concepts` | Literal clip-art of the words | Relevant illustrations | Visual metaphors that explain *better than the words*; each scene has one clear idea |
| `variety` | Same layout repeated | Some variety | Archetypes chosen per beat (diagram, scale journey, number, analogy…); rhythm of wide/close, busy/quiet |
| `continuity` | Colours/meaning drift | Mostly consistent | Each concept keeps its colour/shape across the video; recurring motifs pay off |
| `look` | Frames clash | Frames look like one video | One coherent, striking look that suits the topic; each concept looks the same in every frame; the channel's way of explaining (channel/design.md) intact |
| `timing` | Scenes ignore narration | Scenes match beats; motion plans list entrances | Scene lengths suit content; each `motion` plan says what moves during every line on its cue words, the idea acted out to the end of the scene |
| `feasibility` | Needs photoreal / footage | Buildable | Buildable in HyperFrames + kit within budget; any gen b-roll is atmosphere-only and within cap |
| `style_frames` | None | Some scenes | Every scene (except gen b-roll) has a style frame that carries its idea at a glance, and they look like one video. The image model draws them: details it got wrong are the animator's to correct |

Auto-fail: storyboard doesn't cover every beat in order; gen b-roll used for factual content or over
the runtime cap; a diagram that would misrepresent scale or data.
