# Rubric — packaging (`07-package/`, thumbnail, description)

| criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `title` | Generic / clickbait lie | Clear and accurate | Creates a precise curiosity gap the video fully pays off; ≤60 chars; front-loaded |
| `thumbnail` | Busy, unreadable at 160 px | Clean | One focal subject, ≤4 words, max contrast, readable in qa/thumbnail-small.png; complements (not repeats) the title |
| `promise_match` | Misleading | Matches | Title + thumbnail promise exactly what the first 30 s delivers |
| `description` | Bare | Informative | Hook line, chapters (when valid), full sources block, credit line; no keyword stuffing |
| `discoverability` | No search thought | Some keywords | Natural keywords in title/description/tags informed by `yt youtube search` |

Auto-fail: title or thumbnail claims something the video doesn't; thumbnail text unreadable at 160 px;
description missing sources; `contains_synthetic_media` false while the video has photoreal
generative footage of realistic people/places/events.
