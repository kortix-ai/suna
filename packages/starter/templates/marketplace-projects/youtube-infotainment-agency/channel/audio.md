# Channel bible — sound

## Voice (narration)
- A narrator cast per episode to fit its story (ElevenLabs Voice Library, `03-script/voice.json`, chosen by
  the sound-editor within `channel.yaml → voice.casting`). Whoever narrates: warm, curious; ~145–155 wpm;
  smiles on payoffs; never announcer-style.
- Delivery tags (Eleven v3/v4 audio tags) sparingly, ≤1 per beat: `[curious]`, `[thoughtful]`,
  `[excited]`, `[whispers]`, `[laughs softly]`. Never stack tags.

### Pauses — narration must breathe
Eleven v3/v4 ignore SSML `<break>` tags; the pauses are written into the text (ElevenLabs' prompting guide).
Pause marks don't count against the one-tag limit.
- **Ellipsis** (`...`) holds a beat before a payoff: "you're moving at... seventeen hundred kilometres an hour".
- **`[short pause]` / `[long pause]`** at idea boundaries — before a twist, after a question to the viewer.
- **Full stops over commas**: two short sentences breathe; one long comma chain runs together.
- **`pause_after`** on a beat (script) for air after a reveal; beats are separated by `voice.default_pause` anyway.
- Check the result with `bin/yt voice report` — every sentence end and idea turn should show real silence.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, numbers spoken as people say them ("about six times", not
  "5.86×" — the screen can show the precise figure).
- Re-generate only the beats that change (`yt voice` caches by text), then `yt scene retime`.

## Music
- A score, not wallpaper: one cue per act (2–4 per video), each ≤ 5 min (ElevenLabs Music limit).
- House motif: gentle wonder — felt piano, celesta, soft pads, light pizzicato; builds for the
  climax, resolves warmly. Avoid epic trailer drums, EDM drops, lyrics.
- Prompt template: "<mood>, <instruments>, <tempo> bpm, <dynamic arc>, instrumental, no vocals,
  suitable under narration". Example: "curious and playful, felt piano and celesta with soft
  pizzicato strings, 90 bpm, starts sparse and builds gently, instrumental, no vocals".
- Music sits under the voice: ~0.2–0.3 volume, side-chain ducked automatically in `yt render`.

## Sound effects
- Soft, organic, slightly whimsical: pops for entrances, airy whooshes for camera moves, chimes for
  reveals, ticks for counters, low rumbles for scale. 0.5–2 s each.
- One SFX per meaningful visual event at most; silence is a tool. Never under a key sentence.
- Specified in the storyboard (`scenes[].sfx`) against a beat + cue word, generated by `yt sound`.

## Mix
- Master to −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP (two-pass loudnorm in `yt render`).
- Voice is king: if music or SFX mask a word, the mix fails review.
