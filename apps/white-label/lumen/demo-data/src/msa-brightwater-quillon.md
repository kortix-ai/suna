# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 April 2026 between Brightwater Example Holdings Ltd, of 14 Larkspur Quay, Port Ellison (the "Customer"), and Quillon Analytics Inc., of 900 Fenwick Row, Halden City (the "Supplier").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Services" means the data analytics and reporting services described in each Statement of Work signed by the parties.

1.2 "Statement of Work" means a document that describes Services, deliverables, milestones and fees, and that refers to this Agreement.

1.3 "Fees" means the fees payable for the Services under each Statement of Work.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice and each Statement of Work.

2.2 The Supplier shall assign suitably qualified personnel to the Services and shall not replace its lead analyst without the Customer's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

3.2 The Supplier may increase its rates once per contract year by no more than the change in the consumer price index for the previous twelve months.

## 4. Term and Renewal

4.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for an initial term of three (3) years (the "Initial Term").

4.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of one (1) year each, unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 5. Termination

5.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience at any time by giving the Supplier thirty (30) days' written notice. The Supplier has no right to terminate for convenience.

5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach that it does not remedy within thirty (30) days after notice, or if the other party becomes insolvent.

5.3 On termination, the Customer shall pay for Services properly performed up to the date of termination.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All deliverables created for the Customer under a Statement of Work belong to the Customer on payment. The Supplier keeps its pre-existing tools and methods and grants the Customer a perpetual licence to use them as part of the deliverables.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information confidential during this Agreement and for five (5) years after it ends.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 Where the Supplier processes personal data for the Customer, the parties shall sign a data processing agreement before any processing starts.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional manner and that the deliverables will not infringe the rights of any third party.

## 10. Indemnities

10.1 The Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless the Customer against all losses arising from any claim that the deliverables infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party.

10.2 The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier against all losses arising from data or materials the Customer provides that infringe the rights of a third party.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Neither party's liability under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall be limited or capped in amount. Each party is liable for all losses caused by its breach.

11.2 Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## 12. Assignment

12.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that either party may assign it to an affiliate on written notice.

## 13. Governing Law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in New York County.

## 14. General

14.1 This Agreement, with its Statements of Work, is the entire agreement between the parties. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
