# Share Purchase Agreement (Excerpt)

This Share Purchase Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 20 August 2026 between Aster Example Topco Ltd, of 1 Starling Court, Halden City (the "Seller"), and Brightwater Example Holdings Ltd, of 14 Larkspur Quay, Port Ellison (the "Buyer"), for the sale of the entire issued share capital of Aster Example Systems Ltd (the "Company").

## 1. Sale and Purchase

1.1 The Seller shall sell and the Buyer shall buy the Shares with full title guarantee, free from all encumbrances, for the Purchase Price.

## 2. Purchase Price

2.1 The Purchase Price is the amount stated in Schedule 1, payable at Completion, subject to the adjustments in Schedule 4.

## 3. Escrow

3.1 At Completion, the Buyer shall pay fifteen per cent (15%) of the Purchase Price into an escrow account (the "Escrow Amount"), to be held for eighteen (18) months as security for warranty claims.

## 4. Conditions

4.1 Completion is conditional on the Company obtaining the written consent of Meridian Example Foods plc, the Company's key customer, to the change of control of the Company that the sale will cause, as required under the Supply Agreement between the Company and Meridian Example Foods plc.

4.2 If the condition in clause 4.1 is not satisfied by 31 December 2026, either party may end this Agreement by written notice.

## 5. Warranties

5.1 The Seller gives the Warranties in Schedule 3 to the Buyer at the date of this Agreement and at Completion.

5.2 The Buyer may bring a claim for breach of the Warranties only if it notifies the Seller of the claim within thirty-six (36) months after Completion.

## 6. Limitations on the Seller's Liability

6.1 The Seller's total aggregate liability for all claims under this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred per cent (100%) of the Purchase Price.

6.2 The Seller is not liable for a claim unless the amount of that claim exceeds the de minimis amount stated in Schedule 5.

## 7. Restrictive Covenants

7.1 For three (3) years after Completion, the Seller shall not carry on a business that competes with the Company.

## 8. Assignment

8.1 The Buyer may assign the benefit of this Agreement to a member of its group or to any bank providing finance for the acquisition. The Seller may not assign this Agreement.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
