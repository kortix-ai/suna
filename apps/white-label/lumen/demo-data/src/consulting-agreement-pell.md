# Consulting Agreement

This Consulting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 July 2026 between Brightwater Example Holdings Ltd, of 14 Larkspur Quay, Port Ellison (the "Client"), and Pell Example Consulting LLC, of 61 Juniper Hollow, Fairmarsh (the "Consultant").

## 1. Services

1.1 The Consultant shall advise the Client on its warehouse automation strategy, as described in Exhibit A (the "Services").

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for twelve (12) months unless ended earlier under clause 6.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Client shall pay the Consultant the daily rate in Exhibit A. The Consultant shall invoice monthly in arrears.

3.2 The Client shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of receipt (net 60).

## 4. Independent Contractor

4.1 The Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 All intellectual property rights in the reports, models and other materials that the Consultant creates in performing the Services ("Work Product") remain the property of the Consultant.

5.2 The Consultant grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Work Product for its internal business purposes.

## 6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by giving the other party fifteen (15) days' written notice.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Consultant shall keep the Client's confidential information confidential during this Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 The Consultant's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid to the Consultant under this Agreement.

## 9. Assignment

9.1 The Consultant may not assign or subcontract this Agreement without the Client's prior written consent.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
