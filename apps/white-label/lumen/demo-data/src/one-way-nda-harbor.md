# Confidentiality Agreement (One-Way)

This Confidentiality Agreement (the "Agreement") is dated 12 January 2026 and is between Harbor Example Freight LLC, of 2 Tidewater Lane, Saltmere ("Harbor"), as the disclosing party, and Brightwater Example Holdings Ltd, of 14 Larkspur Quay, Port Ellison ("Brightwater"), as the receiving party.

## 1. Background

1.1 Harbor intends to share information about its logistics network so that Brightwater can evaluate a possible warehousing partnership (the "Opportunity").

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information that Harbor provides to Brightwater about the Opportunity, whether oral, written or electronic, including route data, rates and customer information.

2.2 Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of Brightwater, that Brightwater already held without restriction, that a third party gives Brightwater lawfully, or that Brightwater develops independently.

## 3. Undertakings

3.1 Brightwater shall keep the Confidential Information confidential, shall use it only to evaluate the Opportunity, and shall disclose it only to its directors, employees and advisers who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality duties.

3.2 Brightwater may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, after giving Harbor notice where lawful.

## 4. Duration

4.1 Brightwater's obligations under this Agreement last for two (2) years from the date of this Agreement.

## 5. Residual Knowledge

5.1 Nothing in this Agreement restricts Brightwater from using general skills, know-how and experience retained in the unaided memory of its personnel, provided that this clause does not permit disclosure of Harbor's Confidential Information.

## 6. Return or Destruction

6.1 Within ten (10) business days of Harbor's written request, Brightwater shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify that it has done so.

## 7. Non-Solicitation

7.1 For twelve (12) months from the date of this Agreement, Brightwater shall not solicit for employment any Harbor employee it met in evaluating the Opportunity. General advertisements are not solicitation.

## 8. Assignment

8.1 Brightwater shall not assign this Agreement without Harbor's prior written consent.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
