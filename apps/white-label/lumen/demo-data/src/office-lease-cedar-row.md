# Lease of Office Premises

This Lease is made on 1 June 2026 between Cedar Row Example Properties LLP, of 40 Cedar Row, Port Ellison (the "Landlord"), and Brightwater Example Holdings Ltd, of 14 Larkspur Quay, Port Ellison (the "Tenant").

## 1. Premises

1.1 The Landlord lets to the Tenant the third and fourth floors of 40 Cedar Row, Port Ellison (the "Premises"), together with the right to use the common parts of the building.

## 2. Term

2.1 The term of this Lease is ten (10) years from 1 June 2026 (the "Term").

## 3. Rent

3.1 The initial annual rent is the amount stated in the Particulars, payable quarterly in advance.

3.2 The annual rent shall increase by eight per cent (8%) on each anniversary of the start of the Term, compounding annually.

## 4. Service Charge

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a fair proportion of the costs the Landlord incurs in running the building's common parts.

## 5. Repair

5.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good and substantial repair and condition, including the structure, roof, foundations and exterior walls of the building so far as they form part of or serve the Premises.

5.2 The Tenant shall decorate the interior of the Premises in the last year of the Term.

## 6. Use

6.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices.

## 7. Alienation

7.1 The Tenant shall not assign, underlet or part with possession of the whole or any part of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 8. Break Clause

8.1 The Tenant may end this Lease on the fifth (5th) anniversary of the start of the Term by giving the Landlord at least six (6) months' prior written notice, provided that the Tenant has paid all rent due up to the break date.

## 9. Insurance

9.1 The Landlord shall insure the building against the usual risks. The Tenant shall reimburse the Landlord for a fair proportion of the premium.

## 10. Landlord's Covenants

10.1 The Landlord shall allow the Tenant quiet enjoyment of the Premises without interruption by the Landlord.

## 11. Governing Law

11.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
