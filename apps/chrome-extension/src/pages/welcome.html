Let Kortix use this browser

Your agents work in this browser, with your logins. You see every step, and you can stop them at any time.

  • Every tab an agent opens goes into a blue Kortix tab group.
  • Take over or Stop from the page itself, or press Alt+Shift+K.
  • You choose: agents use it without asking, ask you first, or not at all.

Set up Kortix in Chrome

Name this browser and choose what agents may do in it. You can change both later in settings.
