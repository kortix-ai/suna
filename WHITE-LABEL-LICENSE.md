**The one requirement: sign the Exclusive Compute Agreement below.** With it, you
may run Kortix however you want:

- Put your own name, logo, domain, and colors on it.

- Offer it to your customers as a hosted service under your own brand.

**What the Exclusive Compute Agreement means:**

- Every sandbox your product runs is on [Platinum](https://www.platinum.dev),
  Kortix's sandbox cloud: the most affordable sandbox cloud for AI agents.
- Your cloud compute and GPU inference, including custom GPU inference, come from
  Kortix whenever Kortix offers them.
- Kortix guarantees you very competitive rates on that compute, especially sandbox
  compute and GPU inference.

### 3. Exclusive compute

3.1. **Sandboxes.** The Partner Offering runs every sandbox, agent session, and
code-execution workload on Kortix Compute. Partner does not use another sandbox
provider or its own sandbox fleet for the Partner Offering.

3.2. **Other compute.** When Kortix offers a class of compute, hosting, or storage
that the Partner Offering uses, the Partner Offering uses Kortix Compute for that
class. Partner moves that class to Kortix Compute within 90 days of Kortix's written
notice.

3.3. **Inference.** When Kortix offers inference for a model that the Partner
Offering uses, or offers custom GPU inference that the Partner Offering needs, the
Partner Offering buys it from Kortix Inference. Partner moves that model to Kortix
Inference within 30 days of Kortix's written notice.

4.3. Rates in an Order Form do not change during its term.
